NEXVARY MC removes hidden metadata from supported images before you share them.

Features:
• Fully on-device processing
• No uploads and no tracking
• Review detected metadata before cleaning
• Preserve originals and create cleaned copies
• Arabic, English, Turkish, Spanish, German, Italian, French, Urdu, Persian and Russian
• RTL support for Arabic, Urdu and Persian
• Official NEXVARY About and contact page

The F-Droid build contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, Firebase or Google Play Services.
